# AiHarness

# Constitution

**Version:** 0.1

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

The AiHarness Constitution defines the immutable governance principles that regulate all engineering governance activities performed by AiHarness.

These constitutional principles take precedence over all platform implementations, engine specifications, review profiles, rule packs, and operational procedures.

No engineering activity may violate this Constitution.

---

# 2. Constitutional Principles

The following principles are permanent unless superseded through formal constitutional revision.

---

# 3. Governance Principles

### Law 1 — Governance Independence

AiHarness shall remain independent of engineering planning, implementation, and execution.

Governance shall never be influenced by implementation convenience.

---

### Law 2 — Knowledge Authority

Approved and Frozen engineering knowledge is the sole source of engineering truth.

AiHarness evaluates engineering artifacts against authoritative knowledge.

AiHarness shall never redefine engineering intent.

---

### Law 3 — Evidence Before Conclusion

Every engineering finding shall be supported by explicit engineering evidence.

Findings without evidence are prohibited.

---

### Law 4 — Deterministic Governance

Equivalent engineering inputs shall produce equivalent governance outcomes.

Governance shall be reproducible.

---

### Law 5 — Explainable Decisions

Every governance decision shall explain:

* Applicable Rule Packs.
* Supporting Evidence.
* Engineering rationale.
* Resulting conclusion.

Opaque governance decisions are prohibited.

---

# 4. Review Principles

### Law 6 — Review Before Approval

Engineering artifacts shall be reviewed before reaching an Approved or Frozen lifecycle state.

---

### Law 7 — Review Profiles Govern Reviews

Every review shall execute under an approved Review Profile.

Ad-hoc reviews are prohibited.

---

### Law 8 — Rule Packs Govern Validation

Engineering validation shall be performed through approved Rule Packs.

Rules shall remain reusable and independently versioned.

---

### Law 9 — Missing Evidence Is a Valid Result

If required evidence is unavailable, AiHarness shall report Missing Evidence rather than making assumptions.

---

# 5. Knowledge Evolution Principles

### Law 10 — Findings Do Not Modify Knowledge

Findings identify engineering issues.

They do not change engineering knowledge.

---

### Law 11 — Suggestions Require Approval

Engineering Suggestions are recommendations.

Only explicit approval may authorize engineering change.

---

### Law 12 — Knowledge Evolves Through Patches

Approved engineering knowledge shall evolve only through governed Knowledge Patches.

Direct modification of Approved or Frozen knowledge is prohibited.

---

### Law 13 — Re-review After Change

Every approved Knowledge Patch shall trigger a new engineering review before the updated artifact becomes authoritative.

---

# 6. Traceability Principles

### Law 14 — Complete Traceability

Every review, finding, suggestion, patch, and approval shall remain fully traceable.

---

### Law 15 — Immutable Governance History

Completed reviews and governance decisions shall remain immutable.

Corrections shall create new governance artifacts rather than altering historical records.

---

# 7. Lifecycle Principles

### Law 16 — Governance Respects Lifecycle

AiHarness shall honor the lifecycle rules defined by Shared Engineering Services.

Governance shall not bypass lifecycle constraints.

---

### Law 17 — Frozen Artifacts Are Immutable

Frozen artifacts shall never be modified.

Changes require creation of a new artifact version.

---

# 8. Human Authority

### Law 18 — Human Authority Over Architecture

Architectural changes require explicit human approval.

AiHarness may recommend architectural improvements but shall never apply them autonomously.

---

### Law 19 — Human Authority Over Governance

Constitutional principles, Rule Packs, Review Profiles, and governance policies require explicit human approval before adoption or modification.

---

# 9. Platform Independence

### Law 20 — Technology Independence

Governance shall remain independent of programming languages, frameworks, databases, execution platforms, and AI models.

Review outcomes shall depend on engineering quality rather than implementation technology.

---

### Law 21 — Execution Independence

Execution Backends are replaceable.

Governance contracts shall remain stable regardless of the execution platform.

---

# 10. Constitutional Constraints

The following constraints shall always remain true.

* Every review uses a Review Profile.
* Every finding references Evidence.
* Every suggestion references Findings.
* Every patch references approved Suggestions.
* Every approval is traceable.
* Every governance decision is reproducible.
* Every governance artifact follows lifecycle rules.

Violation of these constraints represents a constitutional violation.

---

# 11. Constitutional Supremacy

The Constitution is the highest governance authority within AiHarness.

In the event of conflict, precedence shall be:

1. Constitution
2. Foundation Charter
3. Platform Architecture
4. Domain Model
5. Lifecycle Model
6. Capability Model
7. Review Profiles
8. Rule Packs
9. Engine Specifications

Lower-level documents shall conform to higher-level governance.

---

# 12. Constitutional Amendment

Amendments to this Constitution require:

* Architecture Impact Analysis.
* Human Review.
* Explicit Human Approval.
* Version Increment.
* Traceability Update.
* Revalidation of dependent governance artifacts.

Constitutional amendments are exceptional engineering events.

---

# 13. Relationship to Other Documents

The Foundation Charter defines the purpose and philosophy of AiHarness.

Shared Engineering Services provides the foundational engineering infrastructure.

The Platform Architecture defines the structural organization of AiHarness.

The Domain Model defines governance entities.

The Lifecycle Model defines governance artifact evolution.

The Capability Model defines governance capabilities.

The Review Profile Model, Rule Pack Model, Evidence Model, and Finding & Suggestion Model define the operational governance framework.

All AiHarness artifacts shall comply with this Constitution.
