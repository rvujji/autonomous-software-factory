# 02 – Constitution

**Status:** Authoritative

---

# 1. Purpose

The Constitution defines the immutable engineering rules governing the Bootstrap Autonomous Software Factory (BASF).

These rules establish the boundaries within which BASF may operate. They exist to ensure that every engineering artifact produced by BASF remains trustworthy, reproducible, traceable, and aligned with the Engineering Platform.

No implementation, workflow, prompt, or future capability may violate this Constitution.

---

# 2. Engineering Philosophy

BASF is an engineering execution system.

It does not invent products, architecture, or engineering direction.

Its responsibility is to reliably transform approved engineering knowledge into higher-value engineering artifacts through deterministic execution.

Human intelligence defines engineering intent.

BASF executes engineering intent.

---

# 3. Constitutional Rules

## Rule 1 — Engineering Knowledge is the Source of Truth

All generated artifacts shall originate from approved engineering knowledge.

BASF shall not invent requirements, architecture, domain concepts, or engineering rules that are not supported by authoritative knowledge.

---

## Rule 2 — Artifact Transformation Only

Every BASF engine shall transform one engineering artifact into another engineering artifact.

No engine may perform unrelated responsibilities.

The platform shall remain an artifact transformation pipeline.

---

## Rule 3 — Independent Review

Every generated artifact shall undergo independent review before approval.

Generation and review must remain separate responsibilities.

No engine may review its own output.

---

## Rule 4 — Evidence-Based Repair

Every repair shall be supported by explicit review findings.

Repairs shall not introduce unrelated modifications.

Every modification must be traceable to review evidence or authoritative engineering knowledge.

---

## Rule 5 — Approval Before Freeze

Only approved artifacts may become frozen artifacts.

Rejected or partially reviewed artifacts shall never become part of the authoritative engineering knowledge base.

---

## Rule 6 — Immutable Frozen Artifacts

Frozen artifacts are immutable.

Modifications require a new generation, review, approval, and freeze cycle.

Previous frozen versions shall remain available for historical traceability.

---

## Rule 7 — Full Traceability

Every engineering artifact shall maintain traceability to:

- Source knowledge
- Context
- Generation
- Review
- Findings
- Repairs
- Approval
- Frozen version

No engineering decision shall become anonymous.

---

## Rule 8 — Reproducibility

Given identical inputs, configuration, and engineering knowledge, BASF shall produce equivalent engineering artifacts.

Engineering correctness shall not depend upon randomness.

---

## Rule 9 — Deterministic Execution

BASF shall prefer deterministic engineering decisions over probabilistic behavior whenever possible.

AI assists engineering execution.

AI does not replace engineering discipline.

---

## Rule 10 — Separation of Responsibilities

Each engine owns exactly one engineering capability.

Knowledge loading, context assembly, generation, review, repair, freezing, and storage shall remain independently replaceable.

---

## Rule 11 — Human Authority

Humans remain the ultimate engineering authority.

Only humans may:

- Change engineering standards
- Modify architectural principles
- Define engineering strategy
- Approve constitutional changes

Automation operates within these boundaries.

---

# 4. Ownership

BASF owns engineering execution.

It does not own:

- Product strategy
- Product requirements
- Engineering standards
- Architecture
- Domain models
- Engineering knowledge

Those are owned by the Engineering Platform.

---

# 5. External Dependencies

BASF depends upon:

- Engineering Platform
- Shared engineering standards
- Shared templates
- AiHarness
- Execution Backends

These systems remain independently versioned and independently evolvable.

---

# 6. Engineering Invariants

The following invariants shall always remain true.

- Every artifact has exactly one lifecycle state.
- Every artifact has an identifiable owner.
- Every artifact has a version.
- Every review produces structured findings.
- Every repair references findings.
- Every approval references review evidence.
- Every frozen artifact is reproducible.
- Every execution is logged.

These invariants define the minimum engineering integrity of BASF.

---

# 7. Evolution Rules

New capabilities may be added without modifying existing constitutional principles.

Future BASF versions shall extend the platform through new artifact types and engines while preserving:

- Artifact transformation
- Independent review
- Evidence-based repair
- Traceability
- Reproducibility
- Deterministic execution

Evolution shall increase capability without weakening engineering integrity.

---

# 8. Amendment Policy

The Constitution is intentionally stable.

Changes require explicit architectural approval because modifications affect every engine, workflow, artifact, and engineering process within BASF.

Constitutional amendments shall be rare, deliberate, and fully traceable.
