# Autonomous Software Factory (ASF)

# Constitution

**Version:** 0.1

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

The ASF Constitution defines the immutable principles governing all engineering orchestration performed by the Autonomous Software Factory.

These constitutional laws establish the operating boundaries for planning, orchestration, execution, automation, and release management.

All ASF components, engines, execution adapters, automation workflows, and AI agents shall comply with this Constitution.

---

# 2. Constitutional Principles

The Constitution is the highest authority governing ASF.

No implementation convenience, execution backend limitation, or automation strategy may violate these principles.

---

# 3. Knowledge Principles

### Law 1 — Knowledge is the Source of Construction

Approved engineering knowledge is the sole source from which software shall be constructed.

Engineering implementation shall never invent requirements.

---

### Law 2 — Knowledge Authority

Only Approved or Frozen engineering knowledge may drive planning and implementation unless explicitly authorized.

---

### Law 3 — Knowledge Integrity

ASF shall preserve the integrity of engineering knowledge.

ASF shall never modify engineering knowledge directly.

Knowledge evolution is governed by AiHarness.

---

# 4. Planning Principles

### Law 4 — Planning Before Execution

Implementation shall never begin before an approved engineering plan exists.

---

### Law 5 — Complete Dependency Analysis

Every engineering plan shall be derived from authoritative dependencies.

Execution order shall be determined through dependency analysis.

---

### Law 6 — Deterministic Planning

Equivalent engineering knowledge shall produce equivalent engineering plans.

Planning shall be reproducible.

---

# 5. Task Principles

### Law 7 — Task Packages Govern Execution

Execution Backends shall receive engineering work exclusively through immutable Task Packages.

---

### Law 8 — Context Before Generation

Every Task Package shall contain sufficient engineering context before execution begins.

Execution Backends shall never retrieve engineering knowledge independently.

---

### Law 9 — Immutable Task Packages

Task Packages are immutable once issued.

Changes require generation of a new Task Package.

---

# 6. Execution Principles

### Law 10 — ASF Orchestrates, It Does Not Implement

ASF coordinates engineering execution.

Execution Backends perform implementation.

---

### Law 11 — Execution Independence

Execution Backends are replaceable.

ASF shall remain independent of any specific AI model, IDE, coding assistant, or execution platform.

---

### Law 12 — Deterministic Orchestration

Engineering orchestration shall be deterministic, traceable, and reproducible.

---

# 7. Governance Principles

### Law 13 — Governance Before Acceptance

Engineering outputs shall not become authoritative until successfully governed by AiHarness.

---

### Law 14 — Failed Reviews Trigger Repair

Engineering artifacts that fail governance shall automatically enter the repair workflow.

---

### Law 15 — Governance is Independent

ASF shall never bypass, replace, or influence AiHarness governance decisions.

---

# 8. Release Principles

### Law 16 — Freeze Before Release

Only Frozen engineering artifacts may participate in releases.

---

### Law 17 — Releases are Traceable

Every release shall maintain complete traceability to:

* Engineering Knowledge
* Engineering Plans
* Task Packages
* Implementation Artifacts
* Governance Results

---

# 9. Human Authority

### Law 18 — Human Authority Over Architecture

Architectural changes require explicit human approval.

ASF may recommend changes but shall never apply architectural modifications autonomously.

---

### Law 19 — Human Authority Over Strategy

Planning strategies, orchestration policies, execution policies, and release policies require explicit human approval before adoption or modification.

---

# 10. Automation Principles

### Law 20 — Automate by Default

Engineering activities shall be automated whenever deterministic execution is possible.

Human intervention shall be reserved for:

* Architectural decisions
* Governance approvals
* Exceptional engineering situations

---

### Law 21 — Continuous Engineering

ASF shall continuously progress engineering work until:

* The engineering objective is achieved.
* Human intervention is required.
* Governance prevents further progression.

---

# 11. Constitutional Constraints

The following conditions shall always remain true.

* Every implementation originates from approved knowledge.
* Every execution follows an approved plan.
* Every Task Package is immutable.
* Every implementation is reviewed by AiHarness.
* Every accepted artifact is traceable.
* Every release contains only approved artifacts.

Violation of these constraints represents a constitutional violation.

---

# 12. Constitutional Supremacy

In case of conflict, precedence shall be:

1. ASF Constitution
2. Foundation Charter
3. Platform Architecture
4. Domain Model
5. Lifecycle Model
6. Capability Model
7. Engine Catalog
8. Engine Specifications

Lower-level artifacts shall conform to higher-level governance.

---

# 13. Constitutional Amendment

Amendments to this Constitution require:

* Architecture Impact Analysis.
* Human Review.
* Explicit Human Approval.
* Version Increment.
* Traceability Update.
* Revalidation of dependent architectural artifacts.

Constitutional amendments are exceptional engineering events.

---

# 14. Relationship to Other Documents

The Foundation Charter defines the purpose and philosophy of ASF.

Shared Engineering Services provides the foundational engineering infrastructure consumed by ASF.

AiHarness provides the governance capabilities integrated into ASF workflows.

The Platform Architecture defines the structural organization of ASF.

The Domain Model defines orchestration entities.

The Lifecycle Model defines orchestration artifact evolution.

The Capability Model defines orchestration capabilities.

The Engine Catalog defines the logical engines responsible for implementing those capabilities.

All ASF artifacts shall comply with this Constitution.
