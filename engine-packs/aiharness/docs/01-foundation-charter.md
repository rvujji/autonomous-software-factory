# AiHarness

# Foundation Charter

**Version:** 0.1

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

AiHarness is the Engineering Governance Platform for the Engineering Platform ecosystem.

Its purpose is to ensure that every engineering artifact, decision, implementation, and release conforms to approved knowledge, architectural principles, engineering standards, and governance policies.

AiHarness provides independent engineering governance.

It does not perform engineering planning or software implementation.

---

# 2. Vision

To establish a trustworthy engineering governance platform that continuously improves engineering quality through evidence-based review, deterministic validation, and governed knowledge evolution.

AiHarness shall ensure that engineering decisions are explainable, traceable, reproducible, and continuously verifiable.

---

# 3. Mission

Provide reusable engineering governance capabilities that enable:

* Continuous engineering review.
* Knowledge validation.
* Engineering completeness analysis.
* Architecture compliance.
* Standards enforcement.
* Evidence-based decision making.
* Controlled knowledge evolution.
* Engineering quality assurance.

---

# 4. Scope

AiHarness is responsible for engineering governance.

Its responsibilities include:

* Engineering review.
* Standards validation.
* Rule evaluation.
* Evidence evaluation.
* Finding generation.
* Improvement suggestions.
* Knowledge evolution.
* Governance reporting.

AiHarness does not perform:

* Engineering planning.
* Task generation.
* Software implementation.
* Source code generation.
* Project management.
* Engineering orchestration.

These responsibilities belong to ASF.

---

# 5. Design Philosophy

AiHarness is founded on the following principles.

* Evidence before opinion.
* Governance before implementation.
* Review before approval.
* Traceability before automation.
* Deterministic engineering decisions.
* Continuous quality improvement.
* Explicit human approval for architectural change.
* Reusable governance across engineering domains.

---

# 6. Core Responsibilities

AiHarness provides the following governance capabilities:

* Engineering review.
* Engineering validation.
* Completeness analysis.
* Consistency analysis.
* Standards compliance.
* Evidence evaluation.
* Finding management.
* Suggestion management.
* Knowledge evolution.
* Engineering reporting.

These capabilities shall remain independent of implementation technologies.

---

# 7. Platform Position

Within the Engineering Platform ecosystem:

* Shared Engineering Services provides foundational engineering infrastructure.
* AiHarness provides engineering governance.
* ASF provides engineering orchestration.
* Execution Backends implement engineering work.

AiHarness governs engineering but does not produce engineering artifacts.

---

# 8. Consumers

AiHarness is intended to govern engineering activities performed by:

* ASF
* Human Engineers
* AI Agents
* Execution Backends
* Future Engineering Platforms

Every consumer is evaluated using the same governance principles.

---

# 9. Architectural Principles

AiHarness shall satisfy the following architectural principles.

### Independence

Engineering governance shall remain independent of engineering execution.

---

### Determinism

Equivalent engineering inputs shall produce equivalent governance outcomes.

---

### Explainability

Every governance decision shall be supported by explicit evidence.

---

### Traceability

Every finding, suggestion, and approval shall remain fully traceable.

---

### Extensibility

New governance capabilities shall be introduced through Review Profiles and Rule Packs rather than modifying the governance core.

---

### Technology Independence

Governance rules shall remain independent of programming languages, frameworks, databases, or execution platforms.

---

# 10. Non-Goals

AiHarness is not:

* A Software Factory.
* An AI Coding Assistant.
* A Project Management System.
* A Build System.
* A Deployment Platform.
* A Version Control System.
* A Requirements Authoring Tool.

Its responsibility is engineering governance only.

---

# 11. Governance Philosophy

Engineering quality shall be achieved through iterative governance.

The governance loop consists of:

```text
Review

↓

Evidence

↓

Findings

↓

Suggestions

↓

Approval

↓

Knowledge Patch

↓

Re-review

↓

Freeze
```

This continuous improvement cycle applies to knowledge, architecture, implementation, and engineering processes.

---

# 12. Quality Objectives

AiHarness shall prioritize:

* Correctness.
* Consistency.
* Completeness.
* Explainability.
* Traceability.
* Determinism.
* Maintainability.
* Engineering confidence.

These objectives take precedence over review speed or implementation convenience.

---

# 13. Future Evolution

AiHarness is designed to evolve through additional Review Profiles, Rule Packs, and governance capabilities while preserving its core responsibility as an independent Engineering Governance Platform.

Future enhancements shall strengthen governance without expanding into planning or implementation responsibilities.

---

# 14. Relationship to Other Documents

This Foundation Charter establishes the purpose and guiding principles of AiHarness.

Shared Engineering Services provides the foundational infrastructure used by AiHarness.

The remaining AiHarness documents define the governance architecture, domain model, lifecycle, capabilities, review models, rule models, evidence model, finding model, and engine catalog that realize this vision.

All AiHarness documents shall conform to this charter.
